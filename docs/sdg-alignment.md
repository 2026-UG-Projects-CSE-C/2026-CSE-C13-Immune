# Sustainable Development Goal Alignment: Scope and Evidence

The final-review presentation (page 22 of 27) identifies **three qualitative alignments**. These are interpretive relationships to specific UN goal targets, **not measurements of progress against UN indicators**. IMMUNE Phase I is a local deterministic software demonstrator, not a deployed industrial, commercial, healthcare or educational intervention.

| Goal and official source | Target | Classification | Evidence-supported relationship | Evidence limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal9) **[U1]** | 9.5 (research/technological capabilities); 9.b (domestic research/innovation policy) | **Primary thematic alignment** | Reproducible fault injection, independently checked persisted state, bounded correction, architecture and tests illustrate a software-engineering research capability. | No industrial implementation, infrastructure reliability measurement, policy outcome or SDG indicator improvement |
| [SDG 8 — Decent Work and Economic Growth](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal8) **[U3]** | 8.2 (economic productivity through technological upgrading and innovation) | **Prospective application** | A verification-and-recovery approach might inform more dependable automation of business workflows; the synthetic order task is a demonstrator rather than an enterprise deployment. | No observed productivity, jobs, economic growth, or industrial efficiency effect |
| [SDG 4 — Quality Education](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal4) **[U2]** | 4.4 (relevant technical and vocational skills) | **Secondary educational relevance** | Public source, requirements, architecture, execution scenarios and automated tests may support technical study of software dependability. | No formal educational intervention, learner study or measured learning outcome |

## Claim Discipline

An SDG's relevance to an engineering topic is **not the same as** demonstrated progress toward the goal or its indicators. The relevant UN targets are cited to make the qualitative mapping transparent. In particular, the software's internal event logs do not establish SDG 16 impact, hypothetical healthcare applications do not establish SDG 3 impact, and avoiding duplicate writes in a synthetic task does not demonstrate SDG 12 resource reductions. The presentation appropriately marks SDG 8 as prospective.
